Threefold Bakery in Breckenridge was recently named a best road-trip restaurant by **5280 Magazine**.

The feature spotlights Threefold's food and atmosphere, including the custom mural installation created for the interior.

The commission was designed to reflect place, movement, and shared origin, using layered symbolism and a warm, vibrant palette that supports the bakery's identity.

Press features like this are always a reminder that thoughtful hospitality design can shape how guests remember a space.
